\begin{abstract}
Enterprise AI corpora span heterogeneous, leak-prone containers---emails (\texttt{.eml}, \texttt{.mbox}, Outlook \texttt{.msg}), Office artifacts with hidden revisions or caches (\texttt{.docx} \texttt{<w:delText>}, \texttt{.pptx} hidden slides and speaker notes, \texttt{.xlsx} \texttt{veryHidden} sheets and \texttt{pivotCache}), DevOps configurations (\texttt{.har}, \texttt{.env}, Kubernetes Secrets), call recordings (\texttt{.wav}), and video files (\texttt{.mp4}). Rather than proposing a new neural architecture, \textbf{VeriSieve-MM} makes a systems-integration contribution: consolidating container parsing, archive defense, Unicode anti-evasion, secret scanning, privacy-preserving record linkage (PPRL), spoken inverse text normalization (ITN), and audio/pixel redaction into a single $187{,}087$\,B Rust-to-WebAssembly kernel (\texttt{verisieve\_media.wasm}) audited to have \textbf{zero host imports} and deployed byte-identically across Node.js CLI/MCP and the live browser workbench, backed by fail-closed Learn-Then-Test (LTT) conformal gating. We evaluate VeriSieve-MM against strong third-party baselines across external public benchmarks without synthetic test saturation. On the human-annotated Text Anonymization Benchmark (\textbf{TAB}, $127$ held-out ECHR court rulings, zero \texttt{ai4privacy} overlap), compact $45.8$\,MB INT8 \texttt{gliner-pii-edge-v1.0} raises single-dataset Person F1 from $45.55\%$ (Paper~1 regex) to $74.06\%$ ($+28.51$\,F1) and achieves $99.45\%$ \texttt{DIRECT}-identifier recall at $\tau{=}0.20$, while transparently trailing the $8.7\times$ larger Python server baseline Microsoft Presidio (\texttt{en\_core\_web\_lg}, $400.7$\,MB, $86.27\%$ Person F1, $p{=}0.0002$; $61.37\%$ \texttt{DIRECT} recall). Because compact edge models have finite out-of-distribution accuracy, calibrating our 520-dim LTT gate strictly on \textbf{human TAB labels} (zero scanner circularity, $1{,}999$ held-out paragraphs) certifies monotonic held-out risk control at every $\alpha \in \{0.01..0.20\}$, delivering \textbf{$0.00\%$ Conditional FPR ($0/1{,}623$ leaks) at $30.32\%$ real Safe Yield ($\alpha{=}0.01$)} and \textbf{$1.48\%$ FPR at $33.78\%$ Safe Yield ($\alpha{=}0.05$)}. On $520$ external secret/code files (\texttt{pstu-synthetic-secrets} + Yelp \texttt{test\_data} + $374$ \texttt{MBPP} Python files), \texttt{verisieve\_media.wasm} achieves $72.25\%$ F1 ($100\%$ precision, $0\%$ clean FP) in $33.76$\,ms---$14.6\times$ faster than \texttt{Gitleaks v8.30.1} ($66.36\%$ F1, $493.3$\,ms) and $18.2\times$ faster than Yelp \texttt{detect-secrets} ($86.27\%$ F1, $614.5$\,ms). In an end-to-end spoken-audio loop (offline TTS $\to$ \texttt{faster-whisper} word timestamps $\to$ WASM ITN+GLiNER $\to$ WAV silence $\to$ 2nd-pass \texttt{faster-whisper}), acoustic PII leakage drops from $89.47\%$ to $23.53\%$ ($-76.47\%$ reduction; $9/15$ calls zero leak; $100\%$ non-PII speech retained). Finally, adding a zero-import $128$-bit salted 2-gram Bloom-filter + Soundex PPRL export recovers $100.0\%$ of noisy OCR/ASR cross-modal entity links ($0.0\%$ under exact HMAC) at $0.0\%$ false-link rate across $30$ hard negatives.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

In regulated clinical, legal, and enterprise workflows, preparing unstructured records for retrieval or model post-training cannot rely on cloud APIs. Our companion study of \textbf{VeriSieve} \citep{ardiyansyah2026verisieve} introduced zero-import WebAssembly text cleaning, Dual-CSR document graphs, and Learn-Then-Test (LTT) conformal gatekeeping \citep{angelopoulos2021ltt}. Moving from clean PDFs to full enterprise corpora, however, exposes five systems and evaluation challenges:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Verifiable Zero-Egress Boundary:} Server-side Python pipelines (e.g., Microsoft Presidio \citep{presidio2021}) rely on promise-based deployment isolation, whereas a zero-import WebAssembly binary (\texttt{WebAssembly.Module.imports(mod).length === 0}) provides a load-time checkable capability boundary \citep{haas2017wasm} running identically in Node.js and browser Web Workers.
    \item \textbf{Honest Edge-vs.-Server PII Trade-Offs:} Synthetic benchmarks such as \texttt{ai4privacy-200k} \citep{ai4privacy2023} can flatter small edge NER models whose training data overlaps synthetic templates. True deployment readiness requires evaluating single-dataset Precision-Recall curves on human-annotated, out-of-distribution text such as the Text Anonymization Benchmark (TAB) \citep{pilan2022tab} directly against strong server baselines.
    \item \textbf{Non-Circular Conformal Gatekeeping:} Calibrating an LTT release gate on labels produced by one's own regex scanner only certifies agreement with the regex scanner, not real PII protection. Finite-sample guarantees must be calibrated against external human ground truth and evaluated on both Conditional FPR \emph{and} non-zero Safe Yield.
    \item \textbf{Real End-to-End Media \& Secret Baselines:} Silencing a known timestamp in clean speech is a DSP unit test, not spoken PII redaction, and scoring a secret scanner on self-authored templates saturates artificially. Credible evaluation requires putting a real ASR engine (\texttt{faster-whisper} \citep{radford2023whisper}) in the loop before and after audio redaction, and benchmarking secret detection head-to-head against \texttt{Gitleaks} \citep{gitleaks2025} and Yelp \texttt{detect-secrets} \citep{yelp2018detectsecrets} on external files.
    \item \textbf{Noisy Cross-Modal GDPR Erasure:} Exact salted HMAC pseudonyms fail whenever OCR (\texttt{l}$\to$\texttt{1}, \texttt{O}$\to$\texttt{0}) or ASR (\emph{``Vance''}$\to$\emph{``vans''}) introduces character drift across modalities.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Scope of Novelty.} Individually, DEFLATE inflation, LTT conformal calibration, GLiNER, Bloom-filter PPRL, and regex-plus-NER fusion are established techniques. VeriSieve-MM's contribution is \emph{systems integration under a verifiable zero-import trust boundary}: packaging all seven multimodal sanitization primitives into a single $187{,}087$\,B dependency-free Rust-to-WASM kernel (\texttt{verisieve\_media.wasm}, $24$ exports, $0$ host imports) shared byte-identically between Node/MCP and the live RTIO browser sandbox, governed by fail-closed defaults everywhere (Figure~\ref{fig:mm-arch}).

\subsection{Live Browser Workbench Integration (RTIO Sandbox)}
In the companion RTIO Next.js workbench, \texttt{verisieve\_media.wasm} runs directly in client memory:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Live Stage~3A Redaction \& Forensic Badges:} \texttt{maskTextContextAware} fuses Paper~1 regexes with \texttt{verisieve\_media.wasm} Stage~3A secret/anti-evasion scanning on every uploaded file, while \texttt{CleanTab} displays explicit forensic leak counts (DOCX \texttt{<w:delText>}, PPTX hidden slides/notes, XLSX \texttt{veryHidden}/\texttt{pivotCache}, HAR auth headers, K8s Base64 Secrets, RIFF/MP4 tags).
    \item \textbf{Zero-RAM Browser MP4 Audit \& Interactive Media Studio:} Multi-gigabyte \texttt{.mp4}/\texttt{.mov} files walk top-level and \texttt{moov} ISOBMFF headers via \texttt{Blob.slice()} in $O(1)$ browser RAM without allocating the \texttt{mdat} payload. An interactive \texttt{MediaRedactionStudio} executes WASM WAV waveform silence/beep redaction (with before/after playback and \texttt{.wav} download) and video/image canvas blackout/pixelate/blur (with \texttt{.png} download) entirely client-side.
    \item \textbf{Zero-Import Salted PPRL:} Extending exact HMAC-SHA256 pseudonyms, \texttt{vs\_media\_pprl\_signature} canonicalizes OCR confusables (\texttt{0}$\to$\texttt{o}, \texttt{1}/\texttt{|}$\to$\texttt{l}, \texttt{5}$\to$\texttt{s}), hashes padded character 2-grams into a workspace-salted $128$-bit Bloom filter \citep{schnell2009pprl}, and pairs it with a salted Soundex phonetic digest to link noisy OCR/ASR mentions across modalities without storing plaintext.
\end{itemize}

Two findings stand out cleanly:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Honest Edge-vs.-Server Gap on OOD Legal Text:} On formal ECHR judgments, the $45.8$\,MB edge GLiNER model boosts Person F1 by $+28.51$ points over Paper~1 regex ($74.06\%$ vs.\ $45.55\%$) and reaches $99.45\%$ \texttt{DIRECT}-ID recall ($363/365$, catching court application numbers such as \texttt{36110/97} that Presidio misses at $61.37\%$). However, the $8.7\times$ larger Python server baseline Microsoft Presidio (\texttt{en\_core\_web\_lg}, $400.7$\,MB, $187.3$\,ms/doc) decisively beats the $45.8$\,MB edge model on overall Person F1 ($86.27\%$ vs.\ $74.06\%$, paired permutation $p{=}0.0002$) and All-Maskable F1 ($76.14\%$ vs.\ $41.76\%$) because the compact model over-triggers on generic legal institutions (\emph{``the Court''}, \emph{``the Government''}). By contrast, on synthetic \texttt{ai4privacy-500} (which likely shares synthetic distribution characteristics with \texttt{gliner-pii-edge}'s training data), Hybrid 3A+3B reaches $96.43\%$ Contact/Card/IPv4 recall and $86.13\%$--$91.60\%$ bare-name recall while cutting blind \texttt{PubMedQA} false positives $2.54\times$ below Paper~1's lexicon ($1.87 \to 0.74$/1k words). Reporting TAB directly prevents synthetic-benchmark optimism.
    \item \textbf{Why Fail-Closed Gating Is Essential:} Precisely because a $45$\,MB client-side model cannot match a $400$\,MB server NER stack on every domain, zero-egress pipelines need statistical abstention rather than blind auto-release.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Exp.~4: Real End-to-End Spoken Audio PII Redaction (Whisper $\to$ Redact $\to$ Whisper)}
\label{sec:exp4}

Rather than evaluating WAV silencing on known synthetic windows, we test the full acoustic loop across $15$ spoken customer-service calls synthesized locally via offline Windows SAPI TTS ($16$\,kHz PCM16 WAV, $57$ planted PII tokens across person names, spoken emails, and spoken $10$-digit phone numbers plus surrounding business context):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Pass~1 (ASR):} Local \texttt{faster-whisper} (\texttt{base.en}, INT8 CPU, \texttt{word\_timestamps=True}) transcribes the raw audio, exposing $51/57$ planted PII tokens (\textbf{$89.47\%$ pre-redaction exposure}).
    \item \textbf{Detection \& WASM Waveform Redaction:} \texttt{verisieve\_media.wasm} runs Spoken ITN + Stage~3A + Stage~3B GLiNER-Edge on the live ASR transcript, maps spans to Whisper word timestamps, and silences those windows ($60$\,ms padding, $5$\,ms cosine fade).
    \item \textbf{Pass~2 (Acoustic Leakage Audit):} Re-transcribing every redacted WAV with \texttt{faster-whisper} shows post-redaction PII leakage drops from $89.47\%$ to \textbf{$23.53\%$ ($12/51$ exposed tokens)---a $76.47\%$ real end-to-end reduction} with \textbf{$9/15$ calls achieving zero residual acoustic leakage} and \textbf{$100.0\%$ retention of non-PII business context words} (Figure~\ref{fig:e3}A).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Honest Error Analysis of the $23.53\%$ Residual Audio Leakage.}
Inspecting the $6$ imperfect calls exposes two genuine ASR-in-the-loop failure modes invisible to DSP-only unit tests: (i)~Whisper occasionally fuses spoken emails into a single token with no space around \emph{``at''} (e.g., transcribing \emph{``elena dot vance at acme dot org''} as \texttt{Elena.vansatacme.org}), defeating word-level Spoken ITN; and (ii)~when Whisper inserts spaces before dots (\texttt{david .romeras at vertex .io}), partial-span mapping silences the local-part while leaving the trailing domain audible.

\section{Limitations and Operating Boundaries}
\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Edge vs.\ Server NER Accuracy:} On out-of-distribution formal legal prose (TAB), compact $45.8$\,MB INT8 \texttt{gliner-pii-edge-v1.0} trails the $8.7\times$ larger Python server baseline Microsoft Presidio (\texttt{en\_core\_web\_lg}, $400.7$\,MB) by $12.21$\,F1 on \texttt{PERSON} and $34.38$\,F1 on All-Maskable entities. Deployments needing higher out-of-distribution recall without server egress must either load larger local Apache-2.0 GLiNER models or rely on the non-circular LTT abstention gate.
    \item \textbf{Browser vs.\ Node Runtime Scope:} Zero-import Stage~3A WASM sanitization (30+ secrets, anti-evasion, forensic container parsing, PPRL, WAV redaction, and canvas visual redaction) runs live in the RTIO browser sandbox today. Stage~3B neural ONNX inference (\texttt{gliner-pii-edge}) and local Whisper ASR currently execute in Node/Python local runtimes and fail closed (\texttt{NER\_WEIGHTS\_REQUIRED}, \texttt{ASR\_WEIGHTS\_REQUIRED}) when local weights are absent; browser WebGPU ONNX integration remains planned.
    \item \textbf{Residual ASR Token-Fusion Leakage:} In real Whisper-in-the-loop audio redaction, $23.53\%$ of exposed PII tokens survived due to fused ASR email tokens (\texttt{vansatacme.org}) and punctuation splits, showing that character-level fuzzy email recovery and wider acoustic padding are needed beyond word-level Spoken ITN.
    \item \textbf{Stylized Video-Frame OCR:} Legacy Tesseract.js struggles on low-contrast dark-mode screen recordings where compact ONNX OCR stacks such as PaddleOCR~3.0/PP-OCRv5 \citep{cui2025paddleocr} or Granite-Docling-258M \citep{ibm2025granitedocling} are preferable drop-in providers.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

VeriSieve-MM shows that a verifiable zero-import WebAssembly trust boundary ($187{,}087$\,B, $0$ host imports) can unify enterprise container auditing, competitive sub-$35$\,ms secret scanning, noisy cross-modal PPRL erasure, and client-side audio/visual redaction across Node.js and the browser. Benchmarking honestly on real human-annotated TAB court rulings, external secret corpora against Gitleaks and Yelp \texttt{detect-secrets}, and an end-to-end Whisper audio loop reveals exactly where compact edge models still trail $400$\,MB server NER stacks---and proves that non-circular Ground-Truth LTT gatekeeping bridges that gap with certified $0.00\%$--$1.48\%$ held-out Conditional FPR at $30.3\%$--$33.8\%$ real Safe Yield.
